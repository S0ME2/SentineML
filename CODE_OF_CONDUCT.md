# Code of Conduct

Everyone is welcome to participate in SentinelML. Be respectful in issues, pull
requests, discussions, and other spaces where you represent the project.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code; explain disagreements with evidence.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and accept corrections.
- Respect people's backgrounds, experience, time, and privacy.
- Help keep discussions and contributions accessible to others.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks,
doxxing, and sharing private information without consent are unacceptable.

## Moderation and reporting

Maintainers may remove harmful content, issue warnings, restrict participation,
or ban participants. Responses should match the severity and context of the
behavior. Maintainers follow the same rules and must recuse themselves from
reports involving their own conduct.

Use GitHub's **Report content** or **Block or report user** controls to report abuse
privately to the platform. A dedicated project conduct-reporting contact is not
currently published. Do not repeat private information in a public issue.
Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
